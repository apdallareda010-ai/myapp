Privacy Policy

Effective Date: [Date]

At [Clinic Name] (“we,” “us,” or “our”), we are committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our patients and visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal and health-related information when you visit our clinic, use our website or application, book an appointment, or otherwise interact with our services.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Date of birth
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Home or mailing address
  • Emergency contact information
  • Identification or insurance information where necessary

Health Information

Depending on the services you receive, we may collect:

  • Medical history
  • Symptoms and diagnoses
  • Examination and treatment information
  • Laboratory and test results
  • Medication and prescription information
  • Allergies and other relevant health information
  • Information provided by other healthcare professionals involved in your care

Appointment and Payment Information

  • Appointment dates and times
  • Services received
  • Billing and payment records
  • Insurance claims and related information

Website and Application Information

If you use our website or application, we may automatically collect certain technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and information about how you use our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide medical care and treatment
  • Schedule and manage appointments
  • Maintain accurate medical records
  • Communicate with you about your care
  • Contact you regarding appointments, test results, or treatment
  • Process payments and insurance claims
  • Coordinate care with other healthcare professionals when appropriate
  • Improve the quality and safety of our services
  • Maintain and secure our systems and facilities
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, and professional obligations
  • Respond to emergencies when necessary to protect your health or safety

We will only use your information for legitimate purposes and in accordance with applicable privacy and healthcare laws.

3. Sharing Your Information

We treat patient information as confidential. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary for purposes such as:

  • Providing healthcare and coordinating treatment
  • Processing insurance claims or payments
  • Working with laboratories, pharmacies, hospitals, or other healthcare providers
  • Using service providers who assist us with clinic operations
  • Meeting legal or regulatory requirements
  • Responding to valid legal requests
  • Protecting the health, safety, or rights of patients or others where permitted or required by law

We do not sell patients' personal or medical information.

4. Patient Communications

We may contact you using the contact information you provide for purposes such as appointment reminders, follow-up communications, test results, billing matters, or other healthcare-related communications.

Where permitted, we may also send information about clinic services or general health-related updates. You may opt out of non-essential marketing communications at any time.

5. Medical Records

We maintain patient records in accordance with applicable legal, professional, and medical-record retention requirements. Access to medical records is limited to authorized personnel and healthcare providers who need the information to perform their duties or provide care.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal and health information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. Despite these safeguards, no electronic system or method of transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Third-Party Services

Our clinic may use third-party providers for services such as:

  • Appointment scheduling
  • Electronic medical records
  • Payment processing
  • Laboratory services
  • Insurance processing
  • Website hosting
  • Email or SMS communications

Where these providers handle personal or health information on our behalf, we take reasonable steps to ensure that the information is handled appropriately and securely.

8. Google User Data

If you sign in with Google or connect your Google account to our application, we access only the Google user data that you explicitly authorize through Google's consent screen, and only to provide or improve the features you request in the application.

  • We do not sell Google user data or share it with third parties except as needed to provide the service, comply with law, or with your consent.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google user data unless you give consent, it is necessary for security or support, or the law requires it.
  • Our use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke our access at any time from your Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions) and ask us to delete any data we hold.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to access, correct, or update your personal information, ask about how it is used, withdraw consent for non-essential communications, or request deletion where legally permitted. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

10. Data Retention

We keep personal and health information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy and as required by law or professional obligations. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it securely.

11. Children's Privacy

Information about minors is collected and used only with the involvement of a parent or legal guardian, as needed to provide healthcare services.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
