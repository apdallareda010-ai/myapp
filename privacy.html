Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how n8n YouTube (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application and website.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your Google account through Google sign-in (OAuth), we may access the following, only to the extent you authorize on Google's consent screen:

  • Basic account information, such as your name and email address
  • Information about your YouTube channel, such as channel ID, title, and video metadata (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, publish status)
  • Permission to upload and manage videos on the channel you authorize
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, which let the app act on your behalf until you revoke access

We may also collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, and logs needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app's features: detecting new videos and publishing content to your YouTube channel
  • To authenticate you and keep your connection active
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the service
  • To communicate with you about the service or respond to your requests
  • To comply with applicable laws

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

By using the app, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features you request.
  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, including retargeting or interest-based ads.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as needed to provide the service, to comply with law, or with your consent.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google user data unless you give consent, it is needed for security or support, or the law requires it.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us host and operate the app, only as needed to provide the service and under appropriate confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

We store data only as long as needed to provide the service. OAuth tokens are stored securely and used only to perform the actions you request. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure.
