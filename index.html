n8n YouTube

n8n YouTube is an automation application built on n8n that connects to your YouTube channel (with your permission) to help you manage and publish video content automatically.

What the app does

  • Detects new videos on a channel you choose
  • Prepares and republishes videos to the YouTube channel you authorize
  • Runs automatically on a schedule so you save time on repetitive publishing tasks

Your privacy

The app accesses only the Google and YouTube data you explicitly authorize. Please read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
